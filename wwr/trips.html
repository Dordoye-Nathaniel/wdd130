Our Rafting Trips

Pra River Rapids

A full-day run on the Pra River in southern Ghana, winding through lush forest and lively rapids. Best for confident paddlers who want a real challenge.

Volta River at Akosombo

Paddle Ghana's mightiest river in the shadow of the Akosombo hills. A scenic, lively trip that suits groups and confident beginners.

Ankobra Rainforest Float

A relaxed half-day float on the Ankobra River in western Ghana, with calm water, birdsong, and forest views. Perfect for families and first-timers.
